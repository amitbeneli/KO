01 Flagship / June 2026

MakeSense.

Dugit Beach  /  Sea of Galilee  /  Israel

The biggest electronic music festival Israel has seen in the past five years.

In June 2026, 8,000 people filled Dugit Beach on the northern shore of the Sea of Galilee for ten hours of music on a single main stage, from golden hour over the lake to a night under the lasers.

We chose one stage on purpose. With no clashes and no split crowds, every artist played to the full festival. We built the whole thing around that idea: the stage, the light and sound, the sightlines from the sand and the backstage that looked after every artist from arrival to departure.

The tickets sold to full capacity. Make Sense showed that Israel's electronic audience is ready for festivals of this size again, and that we can deliver them.
